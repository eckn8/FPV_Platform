Community Guidelines

Last updated: August 2026

These guidelines describe what's expected from everyone using FPVBase. They work alongside our Terms of Service — breaking them can lead to content removal or account restrictions, as described in our Moderation Policy.

Keep it FPV-related

FPVBase exists for the FPV community — 3D-printable parts, CAD files, CNC designs, accessories, and related discussion. Off-topic content may be removed.

Respect intellectual property

Only publish content you have the right to share. Don't upload files, images, or designs that infringe someone else's copyright, patent, or trademark (e.g., unlicensed reproductions of copyrighted figures or patented parts). If you believe your work was published without permission, see our takedown procedure.

No harassment or insults

Disagreements happen — keep them respectful. Personal attacks, harassment, hate speech, or targeted insults toward other users are not tolerated.

No spam or advertising

Don't use models, comments, or requests to promote unrelated products, services, or links. Genuine, on-topic sharing of your own FPV-related work is always welcome.

No inappropriate or shocking content

Nudity, violence, and other content unrelated to FPV are not allowed. Uploaded images are automatically checked before publication, and all published content can be reported by the community.

Use appropriate language

Automated filtering blocks certain words in titles, descriptions, and comments. This is a blunt tool by nature — if you believe something was blocked in error (for example, a legitimate FPV term), contact us at contact@fpv-base.com.

Report what concerns you

If you see content or behavior that doesn't belong here, use the Report button — every model and comment has one. Reports are reviewed by moderators, not published publicly.

Consequences

Depending on severity, violations may lead to content removal, a temporary restriction from publishing/commenting, or a permanent ban — see our Moderation Policy for how these decisions are made.
