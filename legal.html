About This Site

FPVBase is a community-driven platform built for FPV pilots, builders, and makers. Its goal is to bring together one place to find, share, and improve resources made for FPV — from 3D-printable parts and CAD files to CNC designs and other community-made resources — organized around the equipment and builds they were made for, instead of scattered across files, old links, and different platforms. The platform grows with its community: anyone can publish a creation, request something that doesn't exist yet, or help improve how resources are organized.
